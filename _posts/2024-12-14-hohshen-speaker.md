## 後端里長伯 - JSDC2024 講者公布 📣📣

![hohshen-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/kaTgvuY.png)

▍後端里長伯 | 後端技術專家

═ 向量搜尋與Embedding Model的進階應用 ═

### 關於講者 #Backend #VectorSearch #Database

後端里長伯以「你的後端大小事，都是我的事！」為理念，專注於後端技術開發與資料庫優化。在向量搜尋與 Embedding Model 領域擁有豐富的實務經驗。

在 JSDC 2024 中，後端里長伯將深入探討如何運用 vector 資料庫與 embedding 模型來解決複雜的問題。從圖搜圖、圖搜文、文搜圖到音搜圖等多模態應用，揭開向量搜索的神秘面紗。透過 Google Multimodal 與 PG Vector 實例，從基礎理論到實際操作，帶領聽眾了解如何建構更強大的資料檢索系統。

機會有限，錯過不在。馬上購票行動，到 JSDC 主年會購票。

## Backend Elder, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![hohshen-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/kaTgvuY.png)

▍Backend Elder | Vector Search & Embedding Model Expert

═ Advanced Applications of Vector Search and Embedding Models ═

### About the Speaker #Backend #VectorSearch #Database

Backend Elder is dedicated to solving backend challenges and optimizing database performance. With extensive experience in vector search and embedding models, he brings practical insights to complex data retrieval problems.

At JSDC 2024, he will delve into how to utilize vector databases and embedding models to solve complex problems. From image-to-image, image-to-text, text-to-image, and audio-to-image search applications, he will unveil the mysteries of vector search. Through examples using Google Multimodal and PG Vector, attendees will learn how to build powerful data retrieval systems from basic theory to practical implementation.
