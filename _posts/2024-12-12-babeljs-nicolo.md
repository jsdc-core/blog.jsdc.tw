## JSDC2024 講者公布 📣📣 - Nicolò Ribaudo

![Nicolo-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/dPmVVyQ.png)

▍Nicolò Ribaudo | Babel.js 維護者 & Igalia 資深開發者

═ Babel.js 開源專案的維護與演進之路 ═

### 關於講者 #Babel.js #JavaScript #OpenSource

Nicolò Ribaudo 是一位熱愛開源與 JavaScript 的開發者。目前在 Igalia 工作，致力於推動網路技術的發展，同時也是 JavaScript 編譯器 Babel 的主要維護者。作為 TC39 委員會代表，他多年來參與了多個 JavaScript 提案的開發。

在 JSDC 2024 中，身為 Babel.js 資深貢獻者的 Nicolò 將分享他在開發過程中的種種經歷。只要是寫過 JavaScript 的開發者，多少都會用過 Babel 這個開源套件。作為 Igalia 的資深員工，他將分享在開發開源專案中遇到的各種挑戰與趣事，同時也會探討如何通過貢獻來推進開源專案的發展。這個議題特別適合所有使用 JavaScript 的開發者以及對開源專案感興趣的朋友。

## Nicolò Ribaudo, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![▍Nicolo-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/dPmVVyQ.png)

▍Nicolò Ribaudo | Babel.js Maintainer & Senior Developer at Igalia

═ The Journey of Maintaining and Evolving Babel.js as an Open Source Project ═

### About the Speaker #Babel.js #JavaScript #OpenSource

Nicolò Ribaudo is a developer who loves open source and JavaScript. He works at Igalia, where he helps push the web forward, and maintains Babel — the JavaScript compiler. As a TC39 delegate, he has helped develop different JavaScript proposals over the years.

At JSDC 2024, Nicolò will share his experiences as a senior contributor to Babel.js. Any developer who has worked with JavaScript has likely used Babel. As a senior member of Igalia, he will discuss the various challenges and interesting moments encountered in developing open source projects, as well as how contributions can help advance the open source community. This topic is particularly relevant for all JavaScript developers and anyone interested in open source projects.
