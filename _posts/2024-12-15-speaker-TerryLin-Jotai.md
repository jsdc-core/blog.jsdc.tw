### 關於講者 #Jotai #React #前端開發

Terry Lin 現任 MediaTek 前端工程師，專注於生成式 AI 平台的開發。過去在 Dcard 工作時，經常看到公司前輩活躍於社群中，長期潛水的他也首次探出水面，嘗試進行技術分享！有人說，分享本身也是自己學習更多的機會，希望能藉此與大家交流��共同成長。

每次寫前端總是被很多狀態管理弄得一個頭兩個大，隨著專案的不停擴大，狀態的管理成為你維護跟開發的夢靨，相信這是很多開發者會碰到的難題。這個議程，來自ＭediaTek的Terry 將分享他怎樣透過Jotai將開發跟狀態管理化繁為簡，更分享他在開發中的實戰經驗，讓大家可以更輕鬆快速的掌握Jotai來做更快速的開發。狀態管理可以很單純，而Terry的分享將會打通你的任督二脈，隨即購票參加JSDC2024，成為更卓越的開發者。

在 JSDC 2024 中，Terry 將帶來「用 Jotai 打造更輕量的 React 狀態管理」的演講。還在為 React 的狀態管理感到頭痛嗎？工具太複雜，還動不動就踩坑？別擔心，其實狀態管理可以很簡單。Jotai 採用原子化的設計理念，不僅讓開發過程變得直覺，也能有效減少心智負擔。本講座將深入淺出地介紹 Jotai 的核心概念，並分享導入團隊的實踐經驗，幫助大家輕鬆掌握這個工具。

## Terry Lin the Jotai, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

▍Terry Lin | MediaTek Frontend Engineer

### About the Speaker #Jotai #React #FrontendDevelopment

Terry Lin is currently a frontend engineer at MediaTek, focusing on the development of generative AI platforms. During his time at Dcard, he often saw his senior colleagues actively participating in the community, which inspired him to step out of his comfort zone and start sharing his technical knowledge. Sharing is also a learning opportunity, and he hopes to engage with others to grow together.

At JSDC 2024, Terry will present "Building Lightweight React State Management with Jotai." Are you struggling with React state management? Are the tools too complex and full of pitfalls? Don't worry, state management can be simple. Jotai's atomic design philosophy makes the development process intuitive and effectively reduces cognitive load. This talk will introduce the core concepts of Jotai in an easy-to-understand manner and share practical experiences from team implementation to help you master this tool effortlessly.
