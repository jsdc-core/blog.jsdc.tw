═ New Generation Web - 我們從前端到微前端的故事 ═

### 關於講者 #MicroFrontend #Architecture #SoftwareQuality

John 是一位深耕 Web 全端開發領域的技術專家，擁有豐富的多語言與框架經驗，近年來專注於研究軟體品質、微前端與微服務的相關技術。他曾任多個新創技術主管，目前效力於矽谷資安獨角獸公司 Netskope，帶領團隊開發 SaaS 平台，結合技術與業務需求打造創新解決方案。

John 同時熱衷於知識分享，曾與多個教學單位合作擔任講師，也在 Medium 平台撰寫大量技術文章，涵蓋��前端到全端開發的多樣議題。他相信團隊的成功比個人的成就更為重要，致力於促進技術成長與協作。

在 JSDC 2024 中，John 將帶來主題為 「New Generation Web - 我們從前端到微前端的故事」 的精彩分享，帶領大家探索從單一架構轉型至微前端的技術旅程，並揭示如何在技術與業務之間取得平衡，成功完成遷移計畫。

## New Generation Web with John 📣📣

▍John | Technical Expert & Engineering Manager

═ New Generation Web - Our Journey from Frontend to Micro-Frontend ═

### About the Speaker #MicroFrontend #Architecture #SoftwareQuality

John is a technical expert specializing in full-stack web development, with extensive experience in multiple programming languages and frameworks. In recent years, he has focused on software quality, micro-frontend architecture, and microservices technologies. Having served as a technical leader in several startups, he currently works at Netskope, a Silicon Valley cybersecurity unicorn, where he leads teams in developing SaaS platforms and creating innovative solutions that bridge technical and business needs.

Passionate about knowledge sharing, John has collaborated with various educational institutions as an instructor and actively contributes technical articles on Medium, covering topics from frontend to full-stack development. He believes team success outweighs individual achievement and is dedicated to fostering technical growth and collaboration.

At JSDC 2024, John will present "New Generation Web - Our Journey from Frontend to Micro-Frontend," guiding attendees through the technical journey of transforming from a monolithic architecture to micro-frontend, while revealing how to balance technical and business considerations for successful migration.
