## KK, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![KK-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/t9hKA5l.png)

▍KK | Digital Nomad Engineer at Talent Fit Technology Co., Ltd.

═ Enhancing 104 HR System's Talent Search Function Through Browser Extensions ═

### About the Speaker #JavaScript #ChromeExtension #HRSystem

KK is a digital nomad engineer currently working at Talent Fit Technology Co., Ltd. He specializes in developing efficiency-enhancing extensions, particularly in optimizing human resource management systems.

At JSDC 2024, KK will demonstrate how to develop browser extensions using JavaScript to enhance the 104 HR system's functionality. The presentation will cover techniques for scraping 104 backend information, implementing blocking button features, and managing blocked candidate lists using headless CRM (airtable). These optimizations help recruiters screen candidates more efficiently and synchronize candidate information across multiple brands.
