## KK - JSDC2024 講者公布 📣📣

![KK-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/t9hKA5l.png)

▍KK | 適才科技股份有限公司 數位遊牧小小工程師

═ 透過擴充功能提升 104 人資系統的查詢人才功能 ═

### 關於講者 #JavaScript #ChromeExtension #HRSystem

徐愷（KK）是一位跨足商業與技術領域的雙棲實業家，從寫程式到探討程式，再到用程式助力他人成功。現任職於適才科技股份有限公司，擔任數位遊牧工程師。他熱衷於技術社群與交流活動，致力於透過科技創造價值，並讓身邊的人都能找到成功的契機。

身為技術實踐者與創新推動者，KK 專注於開發提升工作效率的擴充功能，特別是在人力資源管理系統方面的優化。他擅長用通俗易懂的方式，將技術概念轉化為實際應用，相信科技是實現夢想的橋樑，而社群則是讓創意落地的最佳土壤。

在 JSDC 2024 中，KK 將分享如何運用 JavaScript 開發瀏覽器擴充功能，以增強 104 人資系統的功能。演講將探討如何透過程式化方式爬取 104 後台資訊、實作封鎖按鈕功能，以及如何運用 headless CRM（airtable）進行封鎖名單的管理。這些優化功能可協助招募人員更有效率地篩選人才，並在多個品牌間同步管理候選人資訊。

## KK, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![KK-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/t9hKA5l.png)

▍KK | Digital Nomad Engineer at Talent Fit Technology Co., Ltd.

═ Enhancing 104 HR System's Talent Search Function Through Browser Extensions ═

### About the Speaker #JavaScript #ChromeExtension #HRSystem

Kai Hsu (KK) is a versatile entrepreneur bridging business and technology, evolving from programming to program analysis and ultimately using programming to help others succeed. Currently working as a digital nomad engineer at Talent Fit Technology Co., Ltd., he is passionate about technical communities and knowledge sharing, dedicated to creating value through technology and helping others find opportunities for success.

As a technical practitioner and innovation driver, KK focuses on developing efficiency-enhancing extensions, particularly in optimizing human resource management systems. He excels at transforming technical concepts into practical applications in an easily understandable way, believing that technology is the bridge to realizing dreams and that community is the best foundation for bringing creativity to life.

At JSDC 2024, KK will demonstrate how to develop browser extensions using JavaScript to enhance the 104 HR system's functionality. The presentation will cover techniques for scraping 104 backend information, implementing blocking button features, and managing blocked candidate lists using headless CRM (airtable). These optimizations help recruiters screen candidates more efficiently and synchronize candidate information across multiple brands.
