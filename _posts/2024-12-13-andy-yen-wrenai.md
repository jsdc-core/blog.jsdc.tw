## Andy Yen, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![Andy-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/04YqcEr.png)

▍Andy Yen | Backend Engineer at EasyOpen Technology Co., Ltd.

═ Conversing with Data: Exploring the Open Source and Architecture of WrenAI ═

### About the Speaker #Backend #OpenSource #AI

Andy Yen is a backend engineer who enjoys a comfortable work environment and strives to make his team's work environment more pleasant. He is passionate about the world of open source and databases.

At JSDC 2024, Andy will discuss WrenAI, an open-source Text-to-SQL solution developed by Canner. This tool combines powerful large language models (LLM) with a flexible system architecture, enabling users to interact with databases using natural language. In the data-driven era, this technology provides a simplified path for both developers and non-technical users to query data.

As a developer, writing good SQL to interact with databases is a common challenge, whether it's about performance or join issues. Andy will share how Wren AI, an open-source solution, can simplify the data retrieval process using simple queries. During the session, Andy will delve deeper into the development and architecture of Wren AI, offering insights for PMs, database novices, and efficiency-seeking seniors alike.
