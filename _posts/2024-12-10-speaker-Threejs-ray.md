## JSDC2024 講者公布 📣📣📣 - Ray

![Ray-JSDC2024][https://i.imgur.com/Q87mROX.png]

▍Ray | 全端工程師 & 遊戲開發師

═ Three.js 與當前最新發展、相關生態 ═

### 關於講者 #Three.js #全端開發 #遊戲開發

Ray 是一位充滿創意與熱情的全端工程師，同時也是一名遊戲開發師與設計師。他擁有豐富的技術經驗，專精於 Next.js、Vue 與 Three.js，致力於打造互動性強且引人入勝的網站作品。此外，Ray 曾擔任台灣大學課程網全端工程師，以及 Crypto Arsenal 的前端工程師，積累了多元的開發與設計經驗。

目前，Ray 正在創業的旅途中，結合技術與創新的力量，實現自己的理想。他也熱衷於知識分享，經常在社群平台發表前後端開發、人工智慧及其他與電腦科學相關的內容，同時融入個人對生活與貓咪的熱愛。

在 JSDC 2024 中，Ray 將帶來主題為 「Three.js 與當前最新發展、相關生態」 的演講，分享他對 3D 網頁技術的深入理解與實踐經驗，探索最新的技術發展及其在現實中的應用潛力。這是一場結合技術與創造力的精彩分享，絕對不容錯過！

機會有限，錯過不在。馬上購票行動，到 JSDC 主年會購票。

## Ray the Three.js, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![▍Ray-JSDC2024][https://i.imgur.com/Q87mROX.png]

▍Ray | Full-stack Engineer & Game Developer

═ Three.js and Its Latest Developments & Ecosystem ═

### About the Speaker #Three.js #FullStack #GameDevelopment

Ray is a creative and passionate full-stack engineer, game developer, and designer. With extensive technical experience, he specializes in Next.js, Vue, and Three.js, dedicated to creating highly interactive and engaging web projects. His professional experience includes roles as a full-stack engineer at National Taiwan University's course website and as a frontend engineer at Crypto Arsenal.

Currently on his entrepreneurial journey, Ray combines technology and innovation to realize his vision. He is also passionate about knowledge sharing, regularly posting content about frontend and backend development, artificial intelligence, and other computer science-related topics, while incorporating his love for life and cats.

At JSDC 2024, Ray will present on "Three.js and Its Latest Developments & Ecosystem," sharing his deep understanding and practical experience with 3D web technologies, exploring the latest technological developments and their potential real-world applications. This is an exciting presentation combining technology and creativity that you won't want to miss!
