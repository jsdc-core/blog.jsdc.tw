## JSDC2024 講者公布 📣📣📣 - Ray

![Ray-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/Q87mROX.png)

▍Ray | 全端工程師 & 遊戲開發師

═ 從 ThreeJs 邁向 WebXR： 建構未來網頁的沉浸體驗 ═

### 關於講者 #Three.js #全端開發 #遊戲開發

Ray 是一位熱愛貓貓與互動設計的前端工程師。他目前正經營「ray.realms」個人 Threads，分享各種前端技術與生成式 AI 技術文章。曾在台大資訊組協助開發課程網，目前在 BearDBT 擔任設計工程師。擅長於新奇酷炫的網站開發，正在挑戰生成式 AI 與互動設計的結合創業。

在 JSDC 2024 中，Ray 將帶來「從 ThreeJs 邁向 WebXR： 建構未來網頁的沉浸體驗」的演講。他將快速探索從 Three.js 出發到 WebXR 生態系的廣泛技術場景，分享現有的整合技術框架以及產品來加速開發具沉浸感的互動式應用。此外，演講中也會快速掃描現有的 3D 網頁技術，展示這些工具如何串聯，實現虛擬與現實整合的網頁應用。

## Ray the Three.js, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![▍Ray-JSDC2024][https://i.imgur.com/Q87mROX.png]

▍Ray | Full-stack Engineer & Game Developer

═ From Three.js to WebXR: Building Immersive Web Experiences of the Future ═

### About the Speaker #Three.js #FullStack #GameDevelopment

Ray is a frontend engineer who loves cats and interactive design. He currently runs "ray.realms" on Threads, where he shares articles about frontend technologies and generative AI. Having previously helped develop the course website at National Taiwan University's IT department, he now works as a design engineer at BearDBT. Ray specializes in developing innovative and cutting-edge websites, and is currently pursuing entrepreneurial ventures combining generative AI with interactive design.

At JSDC 2024, Ray will present "From Three.js to WebXR: Building Immersive Web Experiences of the Future." He will quickly explore the broad technical landscape from Three.js to the WebXR ecosystem, sharing existing integration frameworks and products to accelerate the development of immersive interactive applications. Additionally, the presentation will survey current 3D web technologies, demonstrating how these tools can be connected to create web applications that integrate virtual and physical realities.
