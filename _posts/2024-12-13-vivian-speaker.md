## JSDC2024 講者公布 📣📣 - Vivian Yeh

![Vivian-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/HZExbXQ.png)

▍Vivian Yeh | 軟體工程師 & 自媒體經營者

═ 輕鬆串接 Gemini API：從 Google AI Studio 到 Vertex AI in Firebase 的前端實作 ═

### 關於講者 #AI #Frontend #Firebase

Vivian Yeh 是一位擁有獨特背景的軟體工程師，從英文系轉職至科技領域，累積了 3 年的開發經驗。除了技術開發外，她也熱衷於職涯探索與自我成長，同時經營 IG 帳號 @vivian_enlife，分享她的經驗與見解。

在 JSDC 2024 中，Vivian 將分享她在 AI 整合開發方面的實戰經驗。演講內容將涵蓋：

- Gemini 模型的優勢
- Google AI Studio 介紹與實作
- AI 服務在前端實作的隱憂
- Vertex AI in Firebase 介紹與實作
- AI 融入軟體服務時的實作技巧
- 成本評估自動化工具介紹：Google APP Script

這個議題特別適合想要將 AI 功能整合到前端應用的開發者，以及對 Google AI 服務感興趣的朋友。

## Vivian Yeh, JSDC2024 Speaker Announcement 📣📣

![Vivian-JSDC2024](https://i.imgur.com/HZExbXQ.png)

▍Vivian Yeh | Software Engineer & Content Creator

═ Easy Integration with Gemini API: Frontend Implementation from Google AI Studio to Vertex AI in Firebase ═

### About the Speaker #AI #Frontend #Firebase

Vivian Yeh is a software engineer with a unique background, transitioning from English literature to tech with 3 years of development experience. Besides technical development, she is passionate about career exploration and personal growth, sharing her insights through her Instagram account @vivian_enlife.

At JSDC 2024, Vivian will share her practical experience in AI integration development. Her talk will cover:

- Advantages of the Gemini model
- Introduction and implementation of Google AI Studio
- Concerns in frontend AI service implementation
- Introduction and implementation of Vertex AI in Firebase
- Implementation tips for integrating AI into software services
- Introduction to cost evaluation automation tools: Google APP Script

This topic is particularly relevant for developers looking to integrate AI features into frontend applications and those interested in Google AI services.
